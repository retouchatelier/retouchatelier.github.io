Our process

Bright, accurate and safe to publish.

Toys are sold on colour and detail, and bought by parents who compare the photo against what arrives. Saturation that flatters the listing creates the complaint.

1 · Colour accuracy

Toy plastics are highly saturated and cameras push them further. We correct back to the real product so the red truck on screen is the red truck in the box.

2 · Surface cleanup

Mould lines, dust, scuffs and fingerprints are cleaned from plastic, while moulded texture, fabric pile on plush and printed decals stay sharp.

3 · Sets & packaging

Multi-piece sets are aligned and evenly spaced so every component is visible, and box artwork, age marks and safety symbols stay legible.

4 · Consistency QC

Scale, angle, background and shadow are matched across a full range, then checked by a second retoucher so a category page looks orderly.

Will the colours stay true to the actual toy?

Yes, and this matters more in toys than most categories. We correct to your physical sample rather than boosting saturation, because a parent comparing the photo to the box is where returns and bad reviews start.

Can you cut out plush and soft toys cleanly?

Yes. Fur and pile edges are masked rather than cut with a hard path, so the toy keeps its soft outline instead of looking shaved against a white background.

Can you arrange multi-piece sets?

Yes. Components can be repositioned, aligned and evenly spaced so every piece in the set is visible and the value of the box is obvious at a glance.

How long does toy retouching take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours, with seasonal ranges scheduled in batches at an agreed daily volume ahead of peak trading.
