FAQ

White Label Retouching — questions

What is white-label retouching?

We do the post-production and deliver it under your studio or agency's brand — your client never sees us. You keep the relationship; we handle the editing.

Can you match our style guide?

Yes — a dedicated retoucher is matched to your brand standards for consistent output.

Do you sign NDAs?

Absolutely. Confidentiality and NDAs are standard for our studio and agency partners.

How does pricing work?

Volume and monthly-retainer pricing scoped to your throughput — the more you send, the lower the rate.

Will my client ever know you were involved?

No. Files carry no watermark, no branding and no metadata pointing to us, and we never contact your client. Everything is delivered in your naming convention for you to pass on directly.

Can you match our existing house style?

Yes. Send a set of approved images and we build a written spec from them covering crop, white point, shadow style and colour handling, then apply it to every batch so output stays consistent with work you delivered before us.

How quickly can you absorb overflow?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Partners with recurring volume get a reserved daily capacity, which means you can commit to a client deadline before the files even arrive.

How is pricing structured for partners?

Volume and retainer rates, scoped to your monthly throughput. The higher and more predictable the volume, the lower the per-image rate.
