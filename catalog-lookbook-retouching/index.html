Our process

Consistency across hundreds of pages.

A catalogue is not a set of images, it is one continuous document. The failure mode is never a bad photo — it is page 40 not matching page 12.

1 · Reference build

We start from your approved hero images and build a written spec covering grade, contrast, skin tone, white point and shadow, so every retoucher on the job works to one target.

2 · Grade & match

Frames shot across different days, locations and lighting setups are matched to that reference, so a spread shot in daylight sits beside one shot in studio without jarring.

3 · Print & screen output

Files are delivered in sRGB for digital and Adobe RGB or CMYK for press, with soft proofing on request, so the printed catalogue matches the version you signed off on screen.

4 · Spread-level QC

The final check reviews images side by side as they will appear on the page, not individually, which is the only way inconsistency actually shows up before print.

How do you keep a whole catalogue consistent?

We build a written reference spec from your approved hero images covering grade, skin tone, white point and shadow, and every retoucher on the project works to it. The final QC reviews images as spreads rather than one by one.

Can you match images shot on different days or locations?

Yes. This is the core of catalogue work. Frames shot in daylight, in studio and on location are matched to one reference so they sit together on a spread without jarring.

Do you deliver print-ready files?

Yes. Adobe RGB or CMYK with soft proofing on request for press, and sRGB for the digital version, so what you approve on screen is what comes off the press.

How long does a catalogue take?
