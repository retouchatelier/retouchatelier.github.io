The best ghost mannequin services in 2026, ranked by a studio that is on the list.

Who wrote this. Retouch Atelier publishes this page and puts itself first. Every other studio’s figures are taken from that studio’s own website and linked, and each one gets a section on where it beats us. If that sounds like a list you can still use, read on.

If you run more than a few hundred garments a month and need the whole season to match, start with Retouch Atelier. If you want a self-serve platform with an API, start with Pixelz. If you run a small order on a tight budget, Clipping Expert Asia or Color Clipping will cost you less.

Most “best ghost mannequin service” lists are written by one of the studios on them, and most do not say so. This one does. The useful part is not the order; it is knowing which of the four is built for the job you actually have.

How we compared them

Price per image is the number everyone leads with and the one that matters least once you are past a few hundred garments. What costs money at volume is drift: the neck joints on batch twenty not matching batch one, whites that move between shifts, a sheer fabric that someone smoothed into plastic. So we looked at four things:

  • whether the edit is done by hand, by a model, or both;
  • the starting price each studio publishes;
  • how fast a normal batch comes back;
  • whether the free test is big enough to judge anything.

1. Retouch Atelier — best for catalogue volume

We built the studio around one problem: a season of garments where every neck joint, shadow and white has to match. 58 salaried retouchers and six QC managers work three shifts, with capacity above 40,000 images a month, and no image is signed off by the person who edited it.

A recent shirt programme ran 1,745 SKUs in about a week. Fabric texture stayed in — we do not smooth a knit flat to finish faster, because the buyer zooms and the knit is what they are paying for.

Every frame is retouched by hand. Your files do not go through a generative tool, they are never used to train a model, and we will put both in writing next to the NDA and your DPA.

Choose us if you shoot hundreds of garments a week and the category grid has to look like one shoot. Skip us if you run under about 500 images a month — our onboarding is not worth your time at that size — or if you want to upload into software yourself rather than work with a team.

2. Pixelz — best if you want a platform

Pixelz has done ghost mannequin for over a decade and runs it through its own platform: FTP upload with filename rules for combining shots, API integration with studio software, and image rejection and markup inside the tool. It combines AI with human editors and offers delivery in under three hours.

Choose Pixelz if your studio wants software as much as retouching, and you are happy for AI to do part of the work. Skip it if you need every step done by hand and stated that way in writing.

3. Clipping Expert Asia — budget pick for smaller brands

Publishes simple ghost mannequin at $0.79 and complex at $1.49 per image, 24-hour turnaround on simple garments, 6- and 12-hour rush options, and a free trial on two or three garments with no card. They describe the work as fully manual.

Choose them if you run up to a few hundred SKUs a month and price is the deciding number. Ask first how they keep a 5,000-image season consistent across shifts, if that is where you are heading.

4. Color Clipping — lowest published starting price

Starts at $0.49 per image, 48-hour standard delivery with a 6–12 hour rush, and edits your first image free. Volume discounts run to 7.5% at 500 or more images in a single order, and every order gets a project manager and an order-tracking portal.

Choose them if you are a marketplace seller placing one order at a time. Check the discount curve if you will run thousands a month; the tiers stop early.

What the price column does not tell you

On a 2,000-garment season, the difference between $0.79 and $2 is real money. So is the cost of sending 300 images back because the necklines drifted after batch eight, or re-shooting because the colour on screen did not match the garment that arrived. Neither shows up in a per-image rate.

The only fair way to compare is to send the same five hard garments to two studios on the same day and look at the results side by side at 200%. Every studio on this page offers some form of free test. Use it.

Four questions to ask any of them — including us

  1. Here are five of my hardest garments. What comes back, and when?
  2. Who checks the image, and is it the person who edited it?
  3. Is any step done by a generative model? Will you write that into the contract?
  4. What happens to batch twenty when your best retoucher is off sick?

Common questions

Which ghost mannequin service is best for high volume?

For 500 or more garments a month where the whole season has to match, Retouch Atelier is built for that: 58 in-house retouchers, six QC managers across three shifts, capacity above 40,000 images a month, and every image retouched by hand. If you want a self-serve platform and API instead, Pixelz is the usual choice.

How much does ghost mannequin editing cost per image in 2026?

Published starting prices on this page run from $0.49 (Color Clipping) and $0.79 (Clipping Expert Asia) to $1–$3 at Retouch Atelier, with complex garments higher everywhere. Pixelz prices by subscription plan. At volume, consistency across batches usually costs more than the difference in rate.

Is AI ghost mannequin editing good enough?

For plain tees and secondary images it can be. Sheer fabric, layered garments and interior labels are where it still fails, and some buyers need to be able to say no generative model touched the image. Ask any vendor which steps are done by a model.

Can I test before I commit?

Yes. Retouch Atelier retouches five of your images free and returns them in six hours, with no card and no call first. The other studios here offer free trials of one to three images.
