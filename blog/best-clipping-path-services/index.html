1. Retouch Atelier — best when edges have to hold at volume

Every path is drawn by hand and priced on four tiers — simple, medium, complex and multi-path — so plain packaging is not charged at the rate of a watch bracelet or a chain. Choose us if you run hundreds or thousands of cut-outs a month and the category grid has to look clean. Skip us if it is a one-off small order where an AI remover is good enough — it often is.

Choose them if you want cut-outs to flow straight from your studio system.

Path or mask?

Hard edges get a path. Hair, fur, lace and smoke need a mask, which costs more and should. If you are not sure which your products need, our guide on clipping path versus background removal versus masking sets it out.

Common questions

Which clipping path service is best for bulk orders?

For thousands of cut-outs a month that must stay consistent, Retouch Atelier draws every path by hand on four complexity tiers at $0.50–$2 an image. Clipping Path Studio and Color Clipping publish lower simple-path prices; Pixelz suits teams wanting an API.

How much does a clipping path cost per image?

Published prices on this page start at $0.25 (Color Clipping) and $0.29 (Clipping Path Studio simple), with complex paths up to $7. Retouch Atelier charges $0.50–$2 depending on the edge.

Is AI background removal good enough?

For simple shapes and small orders it often is. Chains, straps, holes, reflections and fine hair are where automated cut-outs fail, and at catalogue scale the soft edges add up.

Can I test first?

Retouch Atelier cuts out five images free and returns them in six hours. Color Clipping edits one free image and Clipping Path Studio offers a free trial.
