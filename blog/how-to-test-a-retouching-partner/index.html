How to Test a Retouching Partner Before Outsourcing a Full Catalogue

A portfolio shows what a retoucher can do on a good frame. To find out whether they can handle your catalogue, test whether they can repeat the same finish across batches.

Start with images that represent the work. Include an easy frame, a difficult edge such as sheer fabric, and a product where colour or texture must match a reference. Share an approved edit, your crop and background rules, and the file format and naming your team expects.

Review the batch as a set. Put the returned files side by side. Do the margins, colour and background match? Zoom in on the hardest frames to check stitching, fabric texture or reflective edges. Make sure the files can go into your workflow without manual renaming or re-exporting.

Run the test again before moving live work. Change the garment, colourway or angle. See whether the partner applies the same brief and keeps the details you approved.

For high-volume, recurring and enterprise buyers, Retouch Atelier starts with 15–20 images and your approved references, then runs two more test rounds. You can judge the results across batches before assigning production work.
