If you resell retouching under your own name and need capacity you can quote against, start with Retouch Atelier. For the lowest published starting price, Pixel Retouching. For photographers who want a subscription, Your Editing Team.

A white-label partner has two jobs, and most fail the second one. The first is good retouching. The second is staying invisible: no watermark, no folder name, no metadata, no email signature that reaches your client, and work that looks like your house style rather than theirs. If your client ever learns who did the work, you have lost something you cannot sell back.

1. Retouch Atelier — best for studios reselling at volume

Files come back with no watermark, no folder names and no metadata pointing to us, named the way your client expects. We build a spec sheet from your approved samples — crop, white point, shadow style, colour handling — and apply it every time, so the work reads as yours.

58 retouchers let us hold a reserved daily volume for a partner, so you can promise your client a date before the files arrive. NDA as standard, access limited to the retouchers on your job, files deleted on the schedule you set, and we sign your DPA.

Choose us if you quote deadlines to your own clients and need capacity behind them. Skip us if you want a partner you can name publicly — staying unnamed is the service.

2. Pixel Retouching — lowest published entry price

Unbranded, unmarked files with no supplier trace and no white-label surcharge, from $0.30 an image, 24-hour standard turnaround and three free images. They state more than 80 retouchers and around 8,000 images a day, with a Singapore head office and production in Dhaka.

Choose them if price per image is the main number and your floor is already full.

3. Clipping Path Center — manual work for resellers

Delivery under your brand with no marks on files or correspondence, from $0.39 an image, 24-hour standard, two free images under NDA. They state more than 150 in-house editors in Dhaka and three QC passes, and describe the work as hand-edited.

Choose them if most of your volume is cut-outs and simple product work. Ask for their monthly minimum in writing; it was not filled in on their page when we read it.

4. Your Editing Team — built for photographers

White-label editing pitched as an extension of a photographer’s own team, from $1.00 an image for product and $4.00 for beauty and fashion, with a subscription option for agencies. They list offices in Ohio, Cologne and Mumbai.

Choose them if you are a portrait, newborn or fashion photographer rather than a catalogue studio. Ask about NDAs and turnaround; neither is stated on the page.

How to test a white-label partner properly

  1. Send real client files with your naming convention, and check every returned filename and the metadata.
  2. Send your house style guide and see whether they ask questions about it before starting.
  3. Ask what happens when two of your clients send work on the same day.
  4. Ask who at their end can see your client’s name.

Common questions

What is a white-label retouching partner?

A studio that retouches your clients’ images and returns them unbranded, in your naming and house style, so you deliver the work under your own name. The partner never appears to your client.

Which white-label retouching partner is best for studios and agencies?

For studios reselling at volume with fixed deadlines, Retouch Atelier holds reserved daily capacity, delivers unbranded files in your naming convention and signs NDAs and DPAs. Pixel Retouching and Clipping Path Center publish lower entry prices for simpler work.

Do white-label partners sign NDAs?

Retouch Atelier and Clipping Path Center sign NDAs as standard; Pixel Retouching signs on request. Ask any partner who on their side can see your client’s name.

Can I test a white-label partner first?

Yes. Retouch Atelier retouches five images free and returns them in six hours. Pixel Retouching offers three free images and Clipping Path Center two.
