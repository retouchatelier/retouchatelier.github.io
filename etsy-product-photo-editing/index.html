Thumbnail-first framing

Your first image is judged at postage-stamp size. We crop and scale so the product still reads clearly in the search grid, not just on the listing page.

Honest colour

Colour is matched to the real item rather than pushed. On handmade goods an over-saturated photo is the fastest route to a disappointed review.

Texture preserved

FAQ

Etsy Product Photo Editing — questions

Can you keep the handmade character while cleaning images?

Yes — we brighten and tidy without stripping the authentic, handmade feel.

Do you edit lifestyle and flat-lay shots?

Yes — both are cleaned for a bright, consistent Etsy shop.

Can you make my shop look consistent?

Uniform colour, framing and backgrounds give a cohesive storefront.

Free trial?

Yes — send 5 images for a free test edit.

Can you work with photos taken on a phone?

Yes. Most Etsy sellers shoot on a phone. We correct exposure, white balance, perspective and background clutter, and will tell you honestly if a shot cannot be saved rather than returning something weak.

How long does Etsy photo editing take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Small shops usually send batches of 10 to 50 images, and larger ranges are scheduled at an agreed daily volume.

Will my product still look handmade?

Yes, and that is deliberate. We keep the texture and small irregularities that prove the item is handmade. Over-polished handmade goods look mass produced and undercut the reason buyers shop on Etsy.

Is there a minimum order?

No. We work with single listings as well as full shop refreshes, and you can send one image for a free test edit before committing to anything.
