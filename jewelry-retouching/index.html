Our process

How a jewelry image is finished.

Every piece moves through the same controlled workflow, so a forty-image ring set looks like it was shot and finished in a single sitting.

1 · Deep cleanup

Dust, fibres, fingerprints, glue residue and prong scratches are removed at 100% zoom. On polished gold and silver this is the step that separates catalogue-ready from shot-at-home.

2 · Metal & stone

Metal is evened out and reshaped where the lighting failed, so bands keep their form instead of going flat. Diamonds and coloured stones gain controlled clarity and sparkle without losing facet detail or shifting hue.

3 · Reflection control

Studio reflections, tripod shadows and colour casts thrown by nearby props are tamed or removed, so the piece reads as jewellery rather than a mirror of your set.

4 · Batch QC

A second retoucher reviews the full set for consistent colour, background, crop and shadow before delivery. This is why clients send whole seasons without re-briefing us.

How long does jewelry retouching take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Larger collections are scheduled in batches with an agreed daily volume, and rush delivery is available when a campaign or marketplace deadline is fixed.

What file formats do you accept?

RAW, TIFF, PSD, PNG and JPEG. Send the highest quality file you have. We work from the original and return layered PSD or flattened files in the size and colour profile your store or printer needs.

Yes. On-model work includes skin, hand and nail retouching around the piece, plus reflection and shadow control so the jewellery stays the focus of the frame.

Yes. We sign NDAs on request, restrict file access to the assigned team, and delete or return files on an agreed retention schedule.
