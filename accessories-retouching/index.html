What we deliver

Detail that survives the zoom.

An accessory is usually photographed close and viewed closer. Over-cleaning is the common failure: smooth the grain out of leather and it stops reading as leather.

  • Leather, suede and nubuck with grain preserved
  • Hardware — buckles, zips, chains and clasps — cleaned without flattening
  • Stitching kept crisp rather than blurred at the edge
  • Shape correction that does not slim the product
  • Colour matched to the physical sample
  • Reflection and fingerprint removal on patent and metal
  • Pure-white or transparent backgrounds
  • Consistency across colourways and sizes

Our process

What happens to every accessory image.

Returns on accessories are driven by colour and by shape — a bag that looked slimmer online, a shoe whose tan was warmer on screen.

1 · Clean-up without flattening

Dust, fingerprints and studio reflections are removed at full resolution, so grain, weave and stitching stay intact.

2 · Hardware

Buckles, zips and clasps are cleaned and their highlights rebuilt to one light source, rather than left with whatever the studio reflected.

3 · Shape and symmetry

Handles, straps and soles are straightened and balanced. The silhouette is corrected, never slimmed.

4 · Colour and QC

Colour is matched to your sample and held across the range, then a second retoucher reviews the run before delivery.

FAQ

Accessories Retouching — questions

What counts as accessories retouching?

Post-production for bags, footwear, eyewear, belts, wallets, hats, scarves and small leather goods — cut-out, colour, hardware clean-up, shape correction and consistency across the range.

Will retouching flatten the leather grain?

No. Cleaning is done at full resolution so grain, weave and stitching survive. Smoothing texture away is the most common fault in cheap accessory work.

Can you match two shoes to each other?

Yes. Left and right are matched for angle, length and colour so a pair reads as a pair rather than two photographs.

Do you remove reflections from patent leather and metal?

Yes. Reflections of the studio are removed and replaced with highlights built to a single consistent light.

What turnaround should we expect?

Live work is 24 to 48 hours, and a free test edit comes back within six hours.

Is there a minimum volume?

Around 500 images a month is the point where high-volume studio pricing makes sense. Below that a freelancer or small studio will usually serve you better.
