Masking types

The right mask for the edge.

Masking is what you use when a hard path would destroy the detail that makes a product look real. Different edges need different techniques.

Hair & fur masking

Model hair, fur trims and pet products keep individual strands against the new background, instead of the cut-out helmet look that automated tools produce.

Transparency masking

Glass, bottles, acrylic and sheer fabric let the new background show through at the correct density, so the product still reads as transparent rather than frosted.

Layer & alpha masks

Delivered as editable layer masks or alpha channels, so your team can change backgrounds later without paying for the selection twice.

Hybrid path plus mask

Most real products need both: a pen path for the solid body and a mask for the soft or transparent areas. Combining them is what keeps edges crisp and hair believable.

When to use it

Masking or clipping path?

A simple rule: if you can trace the edge cleanly with a pen, use a path. If the edge is soft, fibrous or see-through, it needs a mask.

  • Model shots with loose hair, flyaways or textured fabric
  • Fur, wool, feather and faux-fur products
  • Glass, bottles and jars — see beverage & bottle retouching
  • Sheer fabric, lace and mesh — see lingerie & swimwear
  • Smoke, steam, liquid splashes and soft shadows
  • Solid, hard-edged products belong in clipping path service instead

Our guide on clipping path versus background removal covers the choice in more depth. Masking is usually ordered alongside background removal, shadow creation and colour correction as part of full product photo editing.

When do I need masking instead of a clipping path?

Whenever the edge is soft, fibrous or transparent: hair, fur, feathers, glass, smoke or sheer fabric. A hard path through those areas produces a cut-out edge that looks obviously edited.

How long does image masking take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Masking is slower per image than a simple path because the work is done by hand, and complex hair or transparency is quoted individually.

Do I get editable masks?

Yes. Files can be delivered as layered PSD with live layer masks or alpha channels, so your designers can drop the product onto a new background at any time without a re-edit.
