Our process

The standard pass on every product image.

Most e-commerce images fail for the same four reasons. This is the sequence we run to remove all of them before a file leaves the studio.

1 · Cleanup

Dust, fingerprints, lint, sensor spots, price stickers and packaging damage are removed. On reflective or dark products this is the difference between premium and amateur.

2 · Colour accuracy

Colour is matched to your physical sample or reference file and held across every angle and variant. Inaccurate colour is the leading driver of avoidable returns.

3 · Cut-out & shadow

Hand-drawn paths or masks, then the shadow decision: keep the original, rebuild a natural one, or add a clean drop shadow so the product sits on the page instead of floating.

4 · Consistency QC

A second retoucher checks the whole set for matching white point, scale, crop and shadow direction. A catalogue should look like one shoot, not fifty.
