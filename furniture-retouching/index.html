Our process

Straight lines and honest materials.

Furniture is large, heavy and photographed close. Lens distortion bends what should be straight, and buyers zoom in on the grain before they spend four figures.

1 · Perspective correction

Wide lenses bow verticals and skew tabletops. Legs, edges and panel lines are straightened so a wardrobe looks square and a table looks level.

2 · Material fidelity

Wood grain, veneer, marble veining, rattan weave and metal brushing are kept sharp. These textures are what justify the price, so they are protected rather than smoothed.

3 · Upholstery work

Fabric and leather are cleaned of lint, transit creases and handling marks, while the folds that show cushion depth and seat comfort stay intact.

4 · Colour & QC

Finish colour is matched to your physical sample and held across every angle and colourway, then a second retoucher checks the run for scale, shadow and crop.

FAQ

Furniture Retouching — questions

Can you straighten furniture and fix perspective?

Yes — line and perspective correction gives furniture a clean, professional look for catalogues.

Do you remove wrinkles from upholstery?

We smooth fabric wrinkles and lint while keeping the material realistic.

Can you place furniture into a room scene?

Yes — we offer clean-cut on white or compositing into lifestyle backgrounds.

What's the cost?

Priced per image by complexity; volume discounts apply. Free test edit available.

Can you fix perspective distortion?

Yes. Wide-angle lenses bow verticals and skew tabletops. We straighten legs, edges and panel lines so the piece looks square, which is what makes furniture read as well made.

Can you change the fabric or finish colour?

Yes. Colourways can be generated from one master shot and matched to your physical swatches, which saves re-shooting every variant. We will say honestly when a texture change needs a real photograph.

Can you clean up a styled room scene?

Yes. Cables, sockets, reflections, clutter and uneven window light are handled, and we can balance mixed daylight and lamp light so the room looks inviting rather than orange.

How long does furniture retouching take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Complex room scenes and large seasonal catalogues are scheduled at an agreed daily volume.
