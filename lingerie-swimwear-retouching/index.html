Our approach

Fabric, fit and discretion.

Lingerie and swimwear are the hardest garments to photograph honestly. The fabric is sheer, the fit is intimate, and the customer is judging both from a single frame.

1 · Sheer & lace

Mesh, lace and sheer panels are masked rather than cut, so the background shows through at the right density and the lace pattern stays intact instead of turning to mush.

2 · Fit & support

Straps are straightened, cups shaped, seams aligned and clips or pins removed. The garment must show how it actually fits, since fit is the main reason this category gets returned.

3 · Natural skin

Skin is cleaned of temporary marks, strap lines and redness with texture preserved. We do not reshape bodies unless you brief it, and we will discuss it first if you do.

4 · Colour & QC

Nude tones and blacks are the two hardest colours to hold. Both are matched to your physical sample and checked across the full size and shade run.

FAQ

Lingerie &amp; Swimwear Retouching — questions

Do you offer natural skin retouching for on-model shots?

Yes — we keep skin realistic and tasteful, removing distractions without over-editing.

Can you clean lace and mesh detail?

Delicate fabric clean-up on lace, mesh and elastic is a specialty.

Ghost mannequin or on-model?

Both are available depending on your product presentation.

Is a free trial available?

Yes — send 5 images for a free test edit.

Can you keep sheer fabric transparent?

Yes. Mesh, lace and sheer panels are masked rather than cut with a hard path, so the background shows through correctly and the lace pattern stays readable instead of blurring into a grey edge.

Do you reshape the model’s body?

Only if you specifically brief it, and we will discuss it with you first. Our default is to correct fit, strap tension and fabric pull rather than alter the body, which is also where most advertising standards now sit.

Can you match nude and skin-tone shades?

Yes. Nude tones are the hardest to reproduce and the most returned when wrong. We match to your physical samples and hold the shade ladder consistent across the range.

Is this work kept confidential?

Yes. NDAs are signed on request, access is limited to the assigned retouchers, and files are deleted or returned on an agreed retention schedule.
