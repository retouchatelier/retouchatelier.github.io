Our process

Food has minutes, not hours.

Ice melts, herbs wilt, sauce dulls and cheese sets. The shot you got is rarely the shot the dish deserved, and retouching is where that gap closes.

1 · Freshness recovery

Wilted herbs are revived, dried edges rebuilt, and gloss restored to sauces and glazes so the dish looks as it did in the first thirty seconds under the lights.

2 · Colour appetite

Greens, reds and browns are the appetite colours, and cameras dull all three. We correct them to look appetising while staying true to what the kitchen actually serves.

3 · Cleanup & styling

Crumbs, drips, fingerprints on plates and stray reflections are removed. Garnish and portion placement can be adjusted so every plate in a menu run matches.

4 · Scene & QC

Backgrounds, props and steam are cleaned or enhanced, then a second retoucher checks the set for consistent white balance, crop and plate scale.

FAQ

Food Photography Retouching — questions

Can you make food look fresher and more vibrant?

Yes — colour, contrast and freshness enhancement is the heart of food retouching, kept natural and appetising.

Do you clean up plating and spills?

We remove crumbs, drips and stray garnish for clean, professional plating.

Can you retouch food packaging too?

Yes — packaging labels and surfaces are cleaned and colour-corrected for retail and delivery apps.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 5 images, retouched free, no commitment.

Can you make food that photographed badly look fresh?

Yes, within reason. Wilted herbs, dried edges and dulled sauces can be recovered, and gloss rebuilt. If a dish has genuinely collapsed we will tell you rather than return something that looks artificial.

Will the food still look like what we serve?

That is the line we hold. We make the dish look its best on its best day, not like a different product. Advertising food that cannot arrive as pictured creates complaints, not sales.

Can you add steam, splash or pour effects?

Yes. These can be cleaned, rebuilt or added, matched to the light direction in your original frame so they read as photographed rather than pasted on.

How long does food retouching take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Menu-wide shoots are scheduled in batches at an agreed daily volume, with rush delivery for campaign deadlines.
