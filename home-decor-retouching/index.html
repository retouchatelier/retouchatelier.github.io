Our process

Texture is the product.

Nobody buys a ceramic vase for its shape alone. They buy the glaze, the weave, the grain — and those are exactly the details a flat product photo loses first.

1 · Surface detail

Glaze variation, ceramic crackle, wood grain, rattan weave and woven textile are protected and sharpened, because texture is what signals quality in decor.

2 · Colour honesty

Neutrals are where cameras drift most. Beiges go pink, greys go blue, and terracotta goes orange. We correct to your physical sample so a set actually coordinates.

3 · Shape & symmetry

Vases, mirrors, frames and lamps are straightened and made symmetrical, with lens distortion corrected so nothing leans on a listing page.

4 · Scene & QC

Styled scenes get clutter, cable and reflection cleanup with balanced window light, then a second retoucher checks the run for consistent tone and crop.

Coverage

Every object in the room.

Packshots for the grid and styled scenes for the campaign, matched so a collection reads as one range.

  • Ceramics, vases and tableware with glaze and crackle preserved
  • Candles, diffusers and glassware — wax texture and transparency kept
  • Cushions, throws and rugs with weave and pile detail intact
  • Mirrors and frames with reflection and glass handling
  • Lighting and lamps, including lit and unlit versions matched
  • Wall art and prints straightened and colour-matched for reproduction

Decor ranges normally ship with background removal, shadow creation and colour correction, ready for Amazon, Shopify or Etsy. Larger pieces go through furniture retouching, interiors through real estate photo editing, and seasonal ranges through catalog & lookbook retouching.

Do you match colours across a collection?

Will texture survive the retouching?

Yes, and protecting it is the point. Glaze, weave and grain are what justify a decor price. Over-cleaned decor looks like a 3D render and undersells the product.

Can you match neutral colours accurately?

Yes. Neutrals are where cameras drift most, turning beige pink or grey blue. We correct to your physical sample so pieces in a collection genuinely coordinate on screen.

Can you clean up styled lifestyle scenes?

Yes. Cables, sockets, dust, reflections and distracting props are removed, and mixed daylight and lamp light is balanced so the room looks warm rather than orange.

How long does home decor retouching take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours, with seasonal collections scheduled in batches at an agreed daily volume.
