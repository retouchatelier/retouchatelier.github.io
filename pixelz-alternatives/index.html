Read this first

When Pixelz is the right answer, and you should stay.

  • You want AI-generated imagery and retouching from one supplier. Digital twins, AI models, image-to-video. We do not offer any of that and are not going to.
  • You want a self-serve platform. Dashboards, in-app markup, order tracking, on-time-delivery reporting. That is a real product and we do not have an equivalent.
  • You need three-hour turnaround on demand. Their expedite is a button. Our standard is 24–48 hours.
  • Your volume is small or occasional. They serve buyers with a handful of images. We turn away anyone under 500 images a month, in writing.

If two or more of those describe you, the rest of this page will not change your mind and should not.

Other alternatives worth knowing

We are not the only one.

  • Your own in-house team — genuinely the right answer when volume is steady, predictable and modest, and you can keep a retoucher busy all week. Outsourcing earns its place at peaks and at scale, not by default.
  • Self-serve editing services — several publish instant quotes and six-hour turnaround from around $0.25 an image. If what you want is to upload files and get a price without a conversation, that model beats ours and we will not pretend otherwise.
  • Automated background-removal tools — for simple cut-outs on simple products at very high volume and very low cost, a tool beats any studio. The question is only what happens to lace, hair, glass and jewellery.
  • Managed studios that publish no price — several of the larger alternatives quote only after a call, which makes them hard to compare on paper. The same comparison for Pixofix, whose published daily capacity is larger than ours.

How to decide without taking anyone's word

Run the same batch through two suppliers.

Take twenty frames from a live job — including the three hardest — and send the identical set to both. Then measure rather than squint.

  • Pixel dimensions across all twenty, from each supplier. One value, or several?
  • Background brightness sampled in the same corner of every frame. 255 on all, or drifting?
  • The hardest frame at 100% zoom. Edges on lace, hair or glass is where suppliers separate.
  • What each quoted, including rush surcharges and extra-format copies.

The method is written out in full, free, and written so you can run it on us: the catalogue consistency standard.

FAQ

Pixelz alternatives — questions

How much does Pixelz cost?

$0.95 per image as a published starting price on the Professional plan, with Enterprise pricing listed as custom. Read off their pricing page on 2 November 2026. Third-party comparison pages quote higher figures for specific services; those were not confirmed on Pixelz's own site and we have not repeated them as fact.

Does Pixelz use AI?

Yes, and they say so plainly. A Specialist Assisted Workflow combining retouchers with automated AI steps, plus AI-generated campaigns, AI models, digital twins and image-to-video. Their FAQ states all generation is done in-house. For most buyers that is a reason to choose them.

When should I stay with Pixelz?

If you want AI-generated imagery from the same supplier, if you want a self-serve platform with dashboards, if you need three-hour turnaround on demand, or if your volume is small. Any two of those and you should stay.

What is the alternative for a buyer who cannot use generative AI at all?

A studio where generation is not in the pipeline. We retouch every frame by hand, never run client files through generative tools, never use client work as training data, and sign your own DPA committing to it — including for any partner studio used at peak.

Are you cheaper than Pixelz?

Not uniformly, and we would rather say so. Our clipping path starts below their entry price and our jewellery work starts above it. We publish the full table so you can compare the service you actually buy rather than a headline number.
