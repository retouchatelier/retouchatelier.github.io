Why it matters

Colour is what drives returns.

Customers cannot touch your product. Colour is the one promise your photograph makes, and it is the promise most often broken between screen and doorstep.

Match to the real item

We correct against your physical swatch, Pantone reference or a supplied sample file, rather than guessing from the camera file. Cameras rarely record fabric or metal colour accurately.

Neutral white balance

Mixed studio and daylight leaves casts that tint the whole frame. We neutralise the cast without stripping the warmth that makes a product look inviting.

Consistency across a set

The same product shot on three days under three lighting setups must arrive as one colour. We match across angles, variants and entire seasons.

Correct colour profile

Delivered in sRGB for web and marketplaces, or Adobe RGB and CMYK for print, so what you approve is what actually publishes.

FAQ

Color Correction Service — questions

Why is accurate product colour important?

Wrong colour drives returns and erodes trust; accurate colour correction keeps the online image true to the product.

Can you match new shots to older ones?

Yes — cross-shoot consistency keeps your catalogue uniform over time.

Do you handle print colour too?

We can prepare colour for both screen and print profiles.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — send 5 images for a free test edit.

Can you match a Pantone or physical swatch?

Yes. Send the Pantone code, a swatch photo shot alongside a grey card, or an approved reference file. We correct to that target and hold it across the whole set.

Do you work from RAW files?

Yes, and RAW gives the best result. There is far more recoverable highlight and shadow detail in a RAW file than a JPEG, which matters most on white backgrounds and dark products.

Can you keep colour consistent across different shoot days?

Yes. This is one of the most common requests. We build a reference from your approved image and match later batches to it, so a catalogue shot over weeks still looks like one session.

Do you deliver files for print as well as web?

Yes. Web files come in sRGB, and print files in Adobe RGB or CMYK with soft proofing on request, so colour holds from screen to press.
