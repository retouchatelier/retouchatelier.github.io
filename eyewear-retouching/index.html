Our process

Lenses are the whole job.

Eyewear is mostly glass and polished acetate, which means it reflects the studio, the camera and the photographer. Getting rid of that without killing the lens is the craft.

1 · Reflection removal

Softboxes, tripods and the photographer are cleared from both lenses. What stays is a controlled highlight that tells the customer the lens is glass, not a flat grey fill.

2 · Lens tint accuracy

Gradient, mirrored, polarised and photochromic lenses each carry a specific tint. We match the real density and colour so a sunglass customer gets what the photograph promised.

3 · Frame & symmetry

Temples are aligned, the frame is straightened, and acetate, metal and rimless joints are cleaned. Hinge screws, nose pads and logo etching stay sharp at thumbnail size.

4 · Shadow & QC

A light grounded shadow keeps the frame from floating, then a second retoucher matches angle, scale and tint across every colourway in the run.

FAQ

Eyewear Retouching — questions

Can you remove reflections from glasses lenses?

Yes — controlled lens-reflection removal is central to eyewear retouching, keeping tints and coatings realistic.

Do you handle mirrored and gradient sunglasses?

Yes — we preserve mirror and gradient effects while cleaning distracting studio reflections.

Can you clean metal and acetate frames?

We remove dust, fingerprints and smudges from both metal and acetate for a premium finish.

Is a free test edit available?

Yes — send one pair and we'll retouch it free so you can judge the quality.

Can you remove reflections from the lenses?

Yes. Studio reflections, tripods and the photographer are removed from both lenses, while a controlled highlight is kept so the lens still reads as glass rather than a flat panel.

Can you keep clear lenses transparent?

Yes. Optical frames are masked rather than cut with a hard path, so the background shows through the lens at the correct density and the frame still looks wearable.

Can you generate all colourways from one shot?

In many cases yes. If the frame shape is identical across colourways we can build the range from one master image, matched to your physical samples. We will tell you when a re-shoot is genuinely needed.

How long does eyewear retouching take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours, with full collections scheduled in batches at an agreed daily volume.
