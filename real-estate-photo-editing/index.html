Our process

Bright, straight and honest.

Property images have one job: get the viewing booked. That means rooms that look bright and true, not rooms that look like a different house when the buyer arrives.

1 · Exposure blending

Interiors are dark and windows are bright, and no single exposure holds both. We blend brackets so the room is lit and the window still shows the view instead of a white rectangle.

2 · Vertical correction

Wide lenses lean walls inward and bow door frames. Verticals are straightened and distortion corrected so rooms read square and properly proportioned.

3 · Colour & cast removal

Mixed daylight, tungsten and LED leave rooms orange or green. We neutralise the cast so walls read as the colour they actually are, then warm the scene just enough to feel inviting.

4 · Cleanup & QC

Bins, cables, cars, reflections and lens flare are removed, TV screens filled, lawns tidied, then a second retoucher checks the set for consistent brightness and colour room to room.

FAQ

Real Estate Photo Editing — questions

Do you offer HDR blending for interiors?

Yes — we blend bracketed exposures for balanced, natural interiors with recovered window views.

Can you replace a grey sky?

Yes — realistic sky replacement makes exteriors far more appealing.

Do you correct crooked verticals?

Yes — perspective and vertical correction is standard for property imagery.

Free trial?

Can you fix blown-out windows?

Yes. We blend your bracketed exposures so the room stays lit and the window keeps its view. If you only shot a single frame we can still recover a lot, and we will tell you honestly what is achievable.

Do you offer virtual staging?

Yes. Empty rooms can be furnished, and cluttered or dated furniture removed. Staged images should be disclosed as virtually staged in the listing, which most portals and regulators now require.

How fast is the turnaround?

Most listing sets are returned within 24 hours, and overnight delivery is standard for agencies shooting during the day. High-volume accounts get reserved daily capacity.

Will the property still look like itself?

Yes, and that matters legally as well as commercially. We brighten, straighten and clean, but we do not remove permanent features or defects. A viewer who feels misled does not make an offer.
