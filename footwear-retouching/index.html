Our process

What every shoe image needs.

Footwear is deceptively hard: a shoe is asymmetric, it rarely stands straight, and the material changes completely from toe to sole.

1 · Shape & symmetry

Shoes lean, sag and settle unevenly. We straighten the silhouette, correct the lean and match left and right so a pair looks like a pair rather than two separate photographs.

2 · Material work

Leather grain, suede nap, mesh weave and patent gloss each need different handling. Creases from wear or packing are removed while the natural fold at the toe box stays.

3 · Sole & stitching

Dust and scuffs are cleaned from midsoles and outsoles, and stitching, eyelets and logos are sharpened so detail survives at thumbnail size.

4 · Shadow & QC

A grounded or floating shadow is added so the shoe sits naturally, then a second retoucher checks the run for matching angle, scale and colour.

FAQ

Footwear Retouching — questions

Can you remove creases and scuff marks from shoes?

Yes — crease, wrinkle and scuff removal is a core part of footwear retouching for a factory-fresh finish.

Do you correct shoe shape and symmetry?

We straighten and balance pairs so left and right match cleanly across your catalogue.

Can you clean suede and textured materials?

Yes — we preserve natural texture while removing dust, lint and marks on suede, canvas and leather.

What does footwear retouching cost?

Custom volume and retainer pricing; complex pairs and material work may be more. Free test edit available.

Can you fix a shoe that leans or looks lopsided?

Yes. Straightening the silhouette and matching left to right is standard on every footwear job. It is the single change that makes a listing look professionally shot.

Do you remove creases from the toe box?

We remove creases caused by wear, packing or handling, and keep the natural fold that shows the shoe is real leather or fabric. Removing every crease makes footwear look like a plastic render.

Can you handle 360 spin sets?

Yes. Every frame is matched for colour, exposure and background so the spin plays smoothly without flicker between frames.

How long does footwear retouching take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours, with seasonal catalogues scheduled in batches at an agreed daily volume.
