Path complexity

Priced by how hard the edge actually is.

A rectangular box and a bicycle wheel are not the same job. We quote against four complexity tiers so you are never charged catalogue rates for simple cut-outs.

Simple

Boxes, books, phones, plain packaging and single-shape products with straight or gently curved edges. Fast, high volume, lowest per-image rate.

Medium

Shoes, bags, watches, furniture and products with a few interior holes — handles, straps, buckles or gaps that need their own paths.

Complex

Jewellery chains, cutlery sets, bicycles, machinery and garments with many interior openings. Dozens of separate paths per image, drawn by hand.

Multi-path

Images where each component must be selectable on its own so your team can recolour a shirt, swap a strap or build variants without re-shooting.

How we work

Drawn by hand, not by one click.

Every path is cut with the pen tool at high zoom. Automated selection tools leave soft, chewed edges that show the moment an image is placed on white or enlarged for zoom.

  • Pen-tool paths at 100% zoom — crisp, printable edges every time
  • Interior paths for every hole, gap and cut-out in the product
  • Delivered as PSD with clipping path, transparent PNG, TIFF or JPEG on white
  • Soft or feathered edges where the product genuinely needs them
  • Hair, fur and fabric edges routed to image masking instead, where masking beats a hard path
  • Bulk catalogues at an agreed daily throughput with batch QC

FAQ

Clipping Path Service — questions

What is a clipping path?

A hand-drawn vector outline around your product that isolates it from the background, so it can be placed on pure white, a transparent PNG or any custom backdrop with clean, precise edges.

What's the difference between clipping path and background removal?

A clipping path is the precise hand-drawn outline used to isolate the product; background removal is the result. For hard, well-defined edges we use clipping paths; for soft edges like hair or fur we use masking — whichever gives the cleanest result.

Can you handle complex edges and multiple clips?

Yes — complex shapes, holes, straps and reflective surfaces, plus multi-clip paths so individual parts can be recoloured or edited separately for colour variants.

How much does a clipping path cost?

Custom volume and retainer pricing, scoped to your monthly image volume. Book a consultation for a quote.

Do you offer a free test edit?

Yes, send 5 images and we'll clip it free so you can check the edge quality first.

How fast is clipping path work?

Standard turnaround is 24 hours, and simple high-volume batches are often back sooner. Large catalogues run at an agreed daily volume, with rush delivery available on request.

What do I receive?

Layered PSD with an embedded clipping path, transparent PNG, TIFF or JPEG on white — whichever your workflow needs. Files can be named to match your SKU sheet.

Do you use the magic wand or automated tools?

No. Every path is drawn by hand with the pen tool. Automated selections leave soft edges and halos that become obvious on white backgrounds and at zoom, which is exactly where listing images get judged.

What about hair, fur or transparent products?

Those need masking rather than a hard path. We route them to our image masking workflow, often combining a path for the solid body with a mask for the soft edges.
