Read this first

When Pixofix is the right answer, and you should stay.

  • Their published daily capacity is nearly three times ours. 5,000+ images a day against our 1,800+. If a peak week needs five thousand frames turned around in a day, that is a question for them and not for us.
  • They will retouch fifty of your images free. Our free test is five. If you want a large sample before you commit, theirs is simply the bigger offer and we are not going to match it.
  • You want AI production and retouching from one supplier. AI product-page visuals, AI fashion shoots, AI video, 3D rendering. We offer none of it and are not going to.
  • Your catalogue is generated rather than photographed. Making AI output consistent across a batch is a service they describe on their site. It is not one we do.

If two or more of those describe you, the rest of this page will not change your mind and should not.

Other alternatives worth knowing

We are not the only one.

  • Your own in-house team — genuinely the right answer when volume is steady, predictable and modest, and you can keep a retoucher busy all week. Outsourcing earns its place at peaks and at scale, not by default. The arithmetic, with the formula.
  • Platform-based services — a self-serve dashboard, order tracking, markup tools and an expedite button. If you want to upload and track without a conversation, that model beats ours. The same comparison for Pixelz.
  • Lower-priced bulk studios — several publish instant quotes from around $0.19 to $0.30 an image. We are not the cheapest and the price index shows exactly where we sit, including the entries below us.
  • Automated background-removal tools — for simple cut-outs on simple products at very high volume and very low cost, a tool beats any studio. The question is only what happens to lace, hair, glass and jewellery.

How to decide without taking anyone's word

Run the same batch through both.

Take twenty frames from a live job — including the three hardest — and send the identical set to each supplier. Then measure rather than squint. Both of us offer a free sample, so this costs you nothing but the files.

  • Pixel dimensions across all twenty, from each supplier. One value, or several?
  • Background brightness sampled in the same corner of every frame. 255 on all, or drifting?
  • The hardest frame at 100% zoom. Edges on lace, hair or glass is where suppliers separate.
  • What each quoted, including rush surcharges and extra-format copies. One of us has already published the answer.
  • Ask each in writing whether generative tools touch your files, and whether your work can be used as training data. Compare the written answers, not the sales call.

The method is written out in full, free, and written so you can run it on us: the catalogue consistency standard.

FAQ

Pixofix alternatives — questions

How much does Pixofix cost?

They do not publish it. There is no pricing page on pixofix.com, no rate in the navigation or footer, and their own comparison table describes cost as scalable with volume without giving a number. Pricing follows a call. Read 9 November 2026. We have not repeated any third-party figure for them as fact.

Are you cheaper than Pixofix?

We cannot tell you, and nor can anyone else, because they do not publish prices. What we can do is publish ours in full so that you can put a number against whatever they quote you: $0.50 for clipping path, $1 for ghost mannequin, $2 for jewellery, with the complete table on the pricing page.

Does Pixofix use AI?

Yes, and they describe it openly. AI visuals, AI fashion photoshoots, AI video, an AI models agency and 3D rendering are core services. On retouching their page states that their retouchers use AI selectively for repetitive steps and precision, that it supports the work rather than replacing it, and that a human runs QA on every image. For most buyers that is a reason to choose them.

What is Pixofix's capacity?

They state 5,000+ images a day, higher on request, and a capability of over 100,000 images a month. That is larger than our published figure of 1,800+ a day across 58 in-house retouchers, and if raw peak volume is your binding constraint you should weigh that honestly.

When should I stay with Pixofix?

If you need five thousand images in a day, if you want AI production and retouching from one supplier, if your catalogue is generated rather than photographed, or if a fifty-image free sample matters to your evaluation. Any two of those and you should stay.

What is the alternative for a buyer who cannot have AI touch their files at all?

A studio where generative tools are not in the pipeline and no AI step sits inside the retouching. We work every frame by hand by a named retoucher, never run client files through generative tools, never use client work as training data, and will sign your own data processing agreement committing to it, including for any partner studio used at peak.

Is Squareshot still operating?

No. Squareshot's own website carries a notice from its founder that the company is closed, read 9 November 2026. Note that Squareshot was a photography studio rather than a retouching vendor, so a retouching studio is not a like-for-like replacement for what it did.
