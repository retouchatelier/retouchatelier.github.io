Packshot Retouching

A packshot is the plain studio shot of a product on a clean background, and packshot retouching is the work of making five thousand of them look as though they were shot in the same hour. Cut-out, colour, crop, shadow and file naming — handled as one set rather than one image at a time.

What we deliver

One standard, applied to the whole run.

A packshot is judged against the packshot beside it, never on its own. Every decision below is made once, written down, and then held across the set.

  • Pure-white or transparent backgrounds
  • Hand-drawn clipping paths, not automatic cut-outs
  • Consistent crop, margin and canvas size
  • Natural, drop or reflection shadow to one spec
  • Colour matched to your reference, not to the frame
  • Dust, fingerprint and reflection clean-up
  • Straightening, symmetry and perspective correction
  • Marketplace-ready exports and file naming

Our process

What happens to every packshot.

Packshot work is unglamorous and unforgiving. Nobody notices it when it is right, and everybody notices the one frame that is wrong.

1 · Cut-out by hand

A pen-tool path follows the true edge. Automatic tools chew corners and leave a halo of the old background, which is visible the moment the product sits on white.

2 · Colour to reference

The product is matched to your swatch, code or physical sample — not to whatever the studio light did that day.

3 · Crop, margin, shadow

Canvas size, margin and shadow are set once and applied to the set. This is what makes a category page look deliberate rather than assembled.

4 · QC before delivery

A second retoucher checks the run against the spec. Files are named to your convention and delivered ready to upload.

Coverage

Packshot retouching across the catalogue.

Packshot retouching covers any product shot flat, on a stand or on a sweep — hard goods, soft goods, glass, metal and packaging. However your studio shoots, the output stays consistent.

  • Apparel and soft goods, flat or on form — see apparel retouching
  • Bags, footwear and small leather goods — see accessories retouching
  • Bottles, jars and packaging where labels must stay legible
  • Reflective metal and glass, where the room has to be removed from the reflection
  • Multi-pack and set shots that must align to one another
  • Invisible mannequin work — see ghost mannequin retouching

Packshot runs normally ship with clipping paths, background removal, shadow creation and colour correction, exported for Amazon or Shopify. For seasonal sets see catalog & lookbook retouching.

FAQ

Packshot Retouching — questions

What is packshot retouching?

Packshot retouching is the post-production of plain studio product shots — cut-out, background, colour, crop and shadow — applied consistently across an entire set rather than to single images.

How many packshots can you handle a month?

Up to 40,000 images a month, with 58 in-house retouchers and 6 QC managers across three shifts.

Do you cut out by hand or with automatic tools?

By hand. A drawn path follows the true edge; automatic cut-outs chew corners and leave a halo that shows immediately on white.

Can you match colour to a physical sample?

Yes. Send a swatch, colour code or reference file and we match to it, then hold that colour across every variant in the set.

What turnaround should we expect?

Live work is 24 to 48 hours. A free test edit comes back within six hours.

Is there a minimum volume?

Below roughly 500 images a month a high-volume studio is usually the wrong choice, including this one. Above that the per-image rate falls as volume rises.
