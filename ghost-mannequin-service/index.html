Our process

How the invisible mannequin is built.

A ghost mannequin image is not one photograph. It is a composite, and the quality depends entirely on how carefully the pieces are joined.

1 · Shot planning

We work from your front shot plus an inside-neck or inverted shot. If you send us your shot list first, we will tell you exactly which angles we need so nothing has to be re-shot later.

2 · Neck joint

The inner label, collar seam and back panel are rebuilt from the second shot so the garment reads as if a body were inside it. This is the step most cheap ghost mannequin work gets wrong.

3 · Shape rebuild

Sleeves, shoulders and hems are reshaped to a natural human line. Wrinkles that read as damage are removed, while the folds that show fabric weight and drape are kept.

4 · Colour & QC

Garment colour is matched to your reference or physical swatch, then a second retoucher checks the run for consistent size, shadow and background before delivery.

FAQ

Ghost Mannequin Service — questions

What is a ghost mannequin service?

It's a photo-editing technique where the mannequin is removed from apparel photos and the inner garment (neckline, cuffs, hem) is reconstructed, giving a clean 3D 'invisible mannequin' look without a model.

How much does ghost mannequin editing cost?

Pricing is scoped to your volume and the complexity of the work. High-volume and monthly retainers get lower rates.

What is the turnaround time?

Standard turnaround is 24–48 hours, with rush delivery available for tight deadlines.

Do you offer a free test?

Yes — send 5 images and we'll edit it free so you can judge the quality before ordering.

What shots do you need from me?

A front shot on the mannequin plus an inside-neck or inverted shot showing the inner collar. For open jackets or trousers we may also ask for a back or inside-waist frame. Send your shot list and we will confirm before you shoot.

How long does ghost mannequin editing take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Seasonal catalogues are scheduled in batches at an agreed daily volume, with rush delivery available for fixed launch dates.

Can you work from flat-lay shots instead?

Flat-lay images can be cleaned and shaped, but a true invisible-mannequin result needs the garment shot on a form. If you only have flat-lay files we will tell you honestly what is achievable before you commit.

Do you keep collections confidential before launch?

Yes. We sign NDAs on request, restrict file access to the assigned team, and delete or return files on an agreed retention schedule.
