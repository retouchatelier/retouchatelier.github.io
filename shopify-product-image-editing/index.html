Images that load fast and convert.

Shopify themes crop, compress and lazy-load your images automatically. Files that ignore that end up mis-cropped on mobile or slow enough to cost you the sale.

Consistent aspect ratio

Every product is delivered to one ratio and one scale, so your collection grid stops looking ragged. Mixed ratios are the most common reason a Shopify store looks unfinished.

Theme-safe framing

Products are centred with enough margin that your theme can crop to square, portrait or wide without cutting off an edge.

Web-optimised output

Files are sized and compressed for fast loading in sRGB, keeping detail where it matters. Faster pages help both conversion and search ranking.

Variant sets

Colour and size variants are generated and matched from a master file, so swatch switching on the product page stays visually consistent.

Shopify Product Image Editing — questions

Can you size images for my Shopify theme?

Yes — we export to your required dimensions and web-optimise for fast loading.

Do you keep images consistent across the store?

Yes — uniform cropping, colour and shadows keep your store cohesive.

Transparent or white backgrounds?

Both — whichever suits your theme and layouts.

Free test edit?

How long does Shopify image editing take?

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Full catalogue migrations run in batches at an agreed daily volume, with rush delivery available for a store launch.

What image size should I use on Shopify?

We deliver square images at 2048 by 2048 pixels as the safe default, which supports theme zoom without slowing the page. If your theme needs a different ratio, tell us and we match it exactly.

Can you keep my whole catalogue consistent?

Yes. One ratio, one scale, one white point and one shadow style across every product, checked by a second retoucher before delivery. This is what makes a collection page look professional.

Do you name files for bulk import?

Yes. Send your SKU sheet or handle convention and files come back named and foldered to match, so a CSV import needs no manual sorting.
