Our approach

Natural skin, not plastic.

The mark of good model retouching is that nobody notices it. We clean up what the day introduced — not the person.

1 · Skin cleanup

Temporary blemishes, redness and flyaway hairs are removed using frequency separation and dodge and burn, so pores and skin texture survive. No blur, no smoothing filter.

2 · Hair & styling

Stray hairs are tidied against the background, parting lines cleaned and volume rebuilt where the light flattened it, keeping individual strands rather than a solid shape.

3 · Garment work

Clothing is where most of the value sits: creases removed, hems straightened, straps and necklines aligned, and colour matched to the actual product.

4 · Consistency QC

Skin tone, exposure and colour are matched across a full shoot so a lookbook or PDP gallery reads as one session, then checked by a second retoucher.

FAQ

Model Retouching — questions

Do you keep skin retouching natural?

Yes — we preserve realistic texture and tone, removing distractions without over-editing.

Can you clean garments on the model?

Wrinkle, lint and thread clean-up on-model is standard for fashion retouching.

Can you grade a full shoot consistently?

Yes — consistent colour grading keeps a lookbook or catalogue cohesive.

Will the skin still look real?

Yes. We work with frequency separation and dodge and burn so pores and natural texture stay intact. Blurring skin is fast and obvious, and it damages brand trust more than an unretouched blemish would.

Do you change body shape?

Only if you specifically ask, and we will discuss it with you first. Our default is to correct posture, garment fit and fabric pull rather than alter the model, which is also where most advertising standards sit.

Can you match skin tones across a whole shoot?

Yes. Skin tone, exposure and white balance are matched across every frame so a gallery or lookbook reads as one session rather than a series of separate shots.

How long does model retouching take?

E-commerce volumes are usually 24 to 48 hours. High-end beauty and campaign work takes longer per image and is quoted individually against your reference.
