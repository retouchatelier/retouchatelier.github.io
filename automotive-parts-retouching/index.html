Our process

Parts sell on clarity, not styling.

A buyer searching for a brake caliper is checking fitment, finish and condition. The image has one job: show exactly what arrives in the box.

1 · Cleanup

Workshop dust, oil marks, packing grease and warehouse scuffs are removed so a new part looks new, without hiding genuine wear on refurbished stock.

2 · Metal & finish

Chrome, alloy, anodised and powder-coated surfaces each reflect differently. We keep the finish honest so a buyer can tell polished from brushed before ordering.

3 · Detail retention

Part numbers, casting marks, thread detail and connector pins stay legible. These are what buyers zoom into to confirm they are ordering the right component.

4 · Consistency QC

Angle, scale, background and shadow are held constant across thousands of SKUs, then checked by a second retoucher so a parts catalogue stays uniform.

Can you handle thousands of SKUs?

Yes. With 30+ retouchers we run large parts catalogues at an agreed daily throughput, with a dedicated team and batch QC so angle, scale and background stay identical across the whole run.

Will part numbers and markings stay readable?

Yes. Part numbers, casting marks and thread detail are protected and sharpened, because those are exactly what a buyer zooms in to verify before ordering.

How do you photograph black parts on white?

Black rubber and dark plastic lose their edge against white. We rebuild edge separation and add a controlled shadow so the part keeps its shape instead of turning into a silhouette.

Do you name files to our parts sheet?

Yes. Send your SKU or part-number convention and files come back named and foldered to match, so bulk upload needs no manual sorting.
